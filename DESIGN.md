## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an ultra-luxurious, calm, and clinical sanctuary designed for high-net-worth feline guardians in Abu Dhabi and the wider Gulf. Blending veterinary-grade biometric precision with hospitality-tier opulence, the aesthetic reflects desert sanctuary evenings: nocturnal oasis depths, crystalline structures, and the warmth of irradiated desert dunes.

The emotional signature is protective, serene, prestigious, and technologically advanced. The design style fuses high-end **Glassmorphism** with refined **Minimalism**: luminous smoked-emerald obsidian surfaces, frosted telemetry panes, and micro-luminescent gold accents evoke heirloom-grade medical technology tailored for royal companion care.

## Colors

The color palette is built around deep, nocturnal oasis tones balanced by radiant golden sands:

- **Canvas & Abyssal Backdrops (`#062820`):** The primary canvas background. A deep obsidian-emerald that reduces eye strain in evening monitoring environments and elevates optical contrast.
- **Primary Elevated Glass (`#0A3A2F`):** Semi-translucent core emerald used across surface containers, telemetry pods, and navigational rails at variable opacities (`rgba(10, 58, 47, 0.45)` to `rgba(10, 58, 47, 0.85)`).
- **Secondary Desert Sand Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Refined, metallic sand accent reserved for critical telemetry indicators, biometric thresholds, interactive states, and luxury insignia.
- **Tertiary Champagne Dune Glow (`#E8C872`):** Lighter, ambient gold for subtle glowing micro-rings, active states, sparklines, and metric highlights.
- **Text & Dune Beige (`#F4EBD9`):** Soft, non-glare off-white delivering optimal contrast on nocturnal emerald backdrops without the harshness of pure white.
- **Status & Diagnostic Tints:**
  - *Optimal Vitals:* `#4ECCA3` (Beryl Emerald)
  - *Observation Required:* `#F39C12` (Desert Topaz)
  - *Clinical Urgent:* `#E74C3C` (Crimson Carnelian)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes geometric precision with organic softness:

- **Display & Telemetry Readouts (`Outfit`):** Used for primary section titles, luxury headlines, and large numerical telemetry readouts. Its wide aperture, immaculate geometric balance, and contemporary cut provide high legibility for rapid diagnostic scanning. Large numerical values use tabular figures (`tnum`) to keep telemetry stable during live vitals polling.
- **Editorial & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for clinical notes, dietary advisories, lifestyle tracking, and inputs. Provides comfortable readability on dark glass panels with generous x-height and natural breathing space.
- **Micro-Indicators & Badges:** Rendered in uppercase with elevated tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to echo high-end horology dial markings and clinical precision instruments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced 12-column fluid grid system across desktop, transitioning smoothly into an 8-column layout on tablet, and a single-column or 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - *Mobile:* `< 768px` (single-column cards, full-width edge telemetry with `1.25rem` outer margins).
  - *Tablet:* `768px – 1199px` (8-column modular layout with `2rem` outer margins and sticky sub-navigation).
  - *Desktop:* `1200px+` (12-column structural grid capped at `1440px` max width, flanked by fluid outer gutters).

Layout rhythms rely on generous horizontal negative space to let crystalline glass containers breathe. Internal component padding follows a strict 4px/8px modular scale, maintaining spatial symmetry inside biometric telemetry pods.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use opaque grey drop shadows. Instead, depth is constructed via **Crystalline Glassmorphism**, layered illumination, and subtle metallic bounding:

- **Base Layer (Surface 0):** Deepest background tone `#062820`, occasionally elevated with a subtle radial gradient radiating gold light from corners (`radial-gradient(ellipse at top right, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08), transparent 60%)`).
- **Telemetry & Card Tier (Surface 1):** Frosted glass container using `background: rgba(10, 58, 47, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and `-webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`. Outer boundary defined by a continuous `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)` or `rgba(244, 235, 217, 0.12)`.
- **Raised Interactive Overlays & Modals (Surface 2):** Elevated modal glass using `background: rgba(10, 58, 47, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, bordered by a `1px` gradient ring fading from `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)` at the top edge to `rgba(10, 58, 47, 0.2)` at the base.
- **Luminescent Telemetry Glow:** Active health indicators, telemetry rings, and target pins project an ambient halo using `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` or `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(78, 204, 163, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature inspired by feline ergonomics and natural dune geometries. Pill-shaped radii form the core structural identity:

- **Interactive CTAs, Badges & Chips:** Formed with continuous fully-rounded pill capsules (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Biometric Pods, Vitals Containers & Panels:** Built using `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to `rounded-3xl` (`2rem` / `32px`). This eliminates all harsh abrasive edges, providing a protective and organic presence.
- **Micro-Indicators & Telemetry Gauges:** Radial concentric circles, pill-profile progress tracks, and smoothly-curved vector sparklines.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Gold Lustre Pill):** High-gloss gradient capsule (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E8C872, #D4AF37)`), text set in `#062820` with `font-weight: 600`. Hover state triggers an ambient gold glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Action (Crystalline Emerald Pill):** Frosted capsule with `background: rgba(10, 58, 47, 0.6)`, text in `#F4EBD9`, framed with a `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`. Hover elevates background luminosity to `rgba(10, 58, 47, 0.85)`.
- **Micro Action:** Pill icon buttons with `backdrop-blur(12px)` and gold borders.

### Telemetry Cards & Biometric Pods
- Frosted glassmorphism background (`rgba(10, 58, 47, 0.45)` with `20px` blur), framed in `1px` delicate gold wireframing (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- Card headers pair uppercase tracked metric labels (`label-sm`) with live pulse micro-indicators (pulsing green/gold LED dots).
- Metrics showcase oversized numbers (`metric-display`) beside inline delta badges showing trends over baseline vitals.

### Biometric Telemetry Rings
- Concentric circular SVG rings depicting hydration, resting heart rate, activity, and sleep recovery.
- Background track set at `rgba(244, 235, 217, 0.08)`; active value track rendered with a luminous gradient from `#D4AF37` to `#E8C872`, rounded end caps, and soft blurred drop-shadow aura.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Ultra-slim pill shapes with `padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Text styled with `badge-micro`. Backgrounds utilize low-opacity tinting (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)` for warning/active, `rgba(78, 204, 163, 0.15)` for nominal status) paired with matching `1px` glowing borders.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Numerical Inputs:** Pill or `rounded-2xl` fields with recessed smoked emerald backgrounds (`rgba(6, 40, 32, 0.7)`), `1px` borders in `rgba(244, 235, 217, 0.15)`, and placeholder text in `rgba(244, 235, 217, 0.4)`. Focus switches border to crisp `#D4AF37` with a `0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular pill controls with custom gold check and ring indicators on dark translucent tracks.
- **Sliders & Range Selectors:** Thin `4px` gold-tinted tracks with circular pill knobs styled in solid `#E8C872` and wrapped in an emerald focus ring.

### Lists & Telemetry Logs
- Borderless list rows separated by hairline gold gradients (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2), transparent)`).
- Time-stamped rows feature monospaced numerical alignments, accompanied by biometric icon markers enclosed in miniature glass discs.
